# Refund Request

Corpify offers a **14-day money-back guarantee before you activate your license key**.

## Why this policy

When you activate your license key, the corporation downloads and installs to your machine. At that point you have full access to everything you paid for. Refunds after activation aren't possible — like returning a downloaded book after you've read it.

**Before activation:** 100% refund, no questions asked.

## How to request

Email **support@corpify.tech** with:

- **Subject line:** `Refund request`
- **Body:**
  - Your order number (from the purchase confirmation email)
  - Email address used at purchase
  - Brief reason (optional but helpful for product improvement)

That's it.

## What happens next

1. We confirm your order
2. Process the refund through **LemonSqueezy.com LLC** (our Merchant of Record)
3. Refund hits your original payment method in 5-10 business days
4. We send confirmation when issued

## Special cases

### "I activated by accident and immediately realized I want a refund"

Email us anyway. We're reasonable. If you activated in the last 24 hours and haven't used the corporation, we usually find a way.

### "The product doesn't work for me"

Before requesting refund, try `docs/faq/troubleshooting.md`. Most install issues resolve quickly with support help. We'd rather fix it than refund it.

### "Fraudulent transaction (someone bought without my consent)"

Email immediately. We'll work with LemonSqueezy to reverse. Also report to your bank.

### "Duplicate purchase"

Send both order numbers. We'll refund the duplicate.

## Chargebacks

**Please contact us first** before filing a chargeback with your bank. We process all refund requests within 48 hours. Filing a chargeback without contacting us:

- Costs us a $25 dispute fee from the bank
- May result in your license being deactivated
- Can be reversed only with significant friction on both sides

We've never refused a legitimate refund request. Always email first.

## After refund

Your license key is deactivated. Future re-purchase requires a new transaction.

## Honest commitment

We want you to be happy with Corpify. If it's not for you, take the refund — no hard feelings, no slow walks, no manipulation. Spending $149-$299 on something that doesn't fit your needs helps no one.

— Corpify Support

## Top 10 questions

### 1. Do I need programming skills?

No. If you can copy-paste and follow steps, you can run Corpify. Beginner-friendly guides are in `docs/05-vibe-coding/`.

### 2. What does Corpify need to run?

- Windows 10/11, macOS 13+, or modern Linux
- Internet connection
- An Anthropic API key (~$20-50/month typical usage — separate from Corpify purchase)

### 3. Is the $149 / $299 a one-time fee?

Yes. No subscription to Corpify ever. You pay your own AI provider (Anthropic) for usage.

### 4. Can I use this on more than one computer?

Each license activates one machine. Buy another license for a second machine.

### 5. Can I use Corpify for my clients (commercial use)?

Yes. Your lifetime license covers commercial use including agency work.

### 6. Will I get updates?

Yes. Free for life. Re-run the installer to refresh your installation.

### 7. What's the difference between Standard and Pro?

- **Standard ($149):** 40 specialists across all core departments. Solid for solo entrepreneurs.
- **Pro ($299):** Standard + 12 additional engineering specialists (Software Architect, AI/ML Engineer, Backend, Frontend, DevOps, Security, QA specialists, etc.) + Voice Control (hold-to-talk dictation).

### 8. How do I get a refund?

Within 14 days of purchase, **before** you activate your license key. See [refund-request.md](refund-request.md).

### 9. Where is my license key?

Check your email (the one you used to purchase). Look for an email from LemonSqueezy or support@corpify.tech. Search subject line for "Corpify" or "license".

### 10. Where can I see all my agents?

After install, your team lives in `~/corpify/.claude/agents/`. Each `.md` file is one specialist. You can also open the Visual Office on port 8002 — see `docs/06-corporate-os/`.

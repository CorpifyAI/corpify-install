# Anthropic Startup Program

## Credit amount

**$5,000 – $25,000** in Claude API credits (varies by application strength).

## Who qualifies

- Building a real product that uses Claude (or plans to)
- Less than 2 years old
- Less than $5M in annual revenue
- Not previously enrolled in this program

You don't need VC funding. Solo founders can apply.

## What to prepare

### 1. Anthropic Console account
If you don't have one yet, sign up at https://console.anthropic.com (free).

### 2. Description of your project (~150 words)
What you're building. Who it's for. How Claude fits in.

**Template:**
> [Product name] is a [type of product] for [target audience]. It [primary value proposition].
> 
> We use Claude as [specific role — analysis, content generation, customer support, etc.].
> Currently [stage — prototype / launched / in beta].
> 
> With these credits, we'd [specific roadmap — improve X, scale to Y users, ship feature Z].

### 3. Founder background (~50 words)
Brief — who you are, what you've built before.

### 4. Website
Your landing page URL. If you don't have one yet, Corpify can vibe-code one in 30 minutes (see `docs/05-vibe-coding/`).

### 5. Current monthly Claude spend (if any)
Honest number. Even $0 is fine — say "currently at exploration phase, expected $X/month at production."

## How to apply

1. Visit https://www.anthropic.com/startups
2. Click "Apply now"
3. Fill the form using your prepared materials
4. Submit

Typical response time: **2–6 weeks**.

## Tips for higher approval odds

- **Specificity beats hype.** "Build a journalism research tool that processes 10k articles/month" beats "revolutionary AI platform."
- **Show real progress.** Even a basic prototype URL counts more than slick decks.
- **Pick your number honestly.** Asking for $25K with no usage history may slow review.
- **Mention if you're a Corpify user** — we're an active customer; that signals serious AI deployment to Anthropic reviewers.

## After approval

- Credit appears in your Anthropic Console (Billing tab)
- Used automatically before your card is charged
- Expires per program terms (usually 12 months — check yours)

## If rejected

- Wait the cooldown (usually 6 months)
- Improve your application (more concrete project, ship something, show metrics)
- Reapply

Rejection isn't permanent. Get better.

# Google Cloud for Startups

## Credit amount

- **Tier 1 (no partner):** Up to **$2,000** in Google Cloud credits
- **Tier 2 (with partner like Y Combinator):** **$25,000** Google Cloud credits + access to Google AI models (Gemini)
- **Tier 2 in second year:** Up to $100,000

The $350K figure sometimes seen in marketing combines Cloud credits + bundled tools (Workspace, marketing assistance, etc.) for select graduates.

## Who qualifies

- **Tier 1:** Almost any early-stage tech startup
- **Tier 2:** Must be in a partnered accelerator/VC program

The accessible tier for solo founders is Tier 1 ($2K).

## What you can use it for

- Gemini API (Google's AI models, comparable to Claude/GPT)
- Hosting on GCP (Cloud Run, App Engine, GCE)
- Databases (Firestore, BigQuery)
- AI/ML services (Vertex AI)

## What to prepare

Same materials as Anthropic. For Google specifically:
- **GCP project** — create one at console.cloud.google.com (free)
- **Use case for Google services** — be specific about which (Gemini? Hosting? Both?)

## How to apply

1. Visit https://cloud.google.com/startup
2. Choose "Apply for Tier 1" (or Tier 2 if you have partner credentials)
3. Fill the form
4. Submit

Typical response: **2–4 weeks**.

## Tips

- **Don't pad with Google services you won't use.** Reviewers check follow-through.
- **Tier 1 ($2K) is easy — apply if you'll touch Google services at all.**
- **Tier 2 ($25K) requires real partnership.** Don't claim YC/Sequoia/etc. unless verifiable.

## Why Google for Corpify users specifically

If you're heavy into Claude (via Corpify), Google Gemini may be secondary. But:
- Gemini is competitive on cost for high-volume use cases
- BigQuery is excellent for analytics workloads
- $2K = ~6-12 months of free Gemini API or many months of Firebase backend

Even as a backup provider, the credits have value.

## After approval

- Credits activate in your GCP billing account
- 1-year expiration (typical) — use them
- Build → spend → renew or migrate before expiry

## If rejected

- Often it's a paperwork issue. Read the rejection email — they usually explain
- Resubmit with fix

## Realistic outcome for solo Corpify owners

Most solo founders get **$2K Google + $5-25K Anthropic + $5K Microsoft + $1K OpenAI = ~$15-35K combined**.

That's many months of free AI usage. Apply systematically. Stack the credits.

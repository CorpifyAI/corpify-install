# Block 10 — Apply for AI Startup Credits (Up to $50,000+)

## What this is

Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft all run startup programs that grant free AI API credits to qualifying founders. Most independent founders can realistically apply for and receive **$5,000–$50,000+ in combined credits** without venture funding. With VC backing, the ceiling rises significantly.

This folder gives you step-by-step guides for each program: who qualifies, what to write in the application, what to attach.

## Realistic expectations

| Program | Typical solo founder | With VC backing |
|---------|---------------------|------------------|
| **Anthropic Startup Program** | $5,000–$25,000 credit | Up to $25,000 |
| **OpenAI for Startups** | $1,000 baseline | Up to $50,000 |
| **Microsoft Founders Hub** | $5,000 Azure credit | Up to $25,000 |
| **Google Cloud for Startups** | $2,000 Tier 1 | $25,000+ Tier 2 |

**Combined realistic range for solo founder: $13,000–$50,000+**

These are credits — meaning free usage up to the dollar amount, **not cash**. They reduce your monthly AI bill until consumed.

## Why these programs exist

Cloud providers want to acquire startups early. If your business grows, you become a long-term paying customer. Free credits are their customer acquisition cost.

You're a good fit if:
- You're building a real product (not just experimenting)
- You can articulate the business in 1-2 sentences
- You'll actually use the credits (active developer / active deployment)
- You don't already have massive credits with the same provider

You're NOT a good fit if:
- You're applying just to grab free stuff with no real project
- You've recently received credits from the same program
- You're a large established company

## Application order — recommended sequence

1. **Anthropic Startup Program** — biggest credit per dollar of effort. Apply first.
2. **Microsoft Founders Hub** — easy approval, fast. Apply second.
3. **OpenAI for Startups** — requires VC affiliation for the big credit; apply if applicable.
4. **Google Cloud for Startups** — Tier 1 ($2K) is easy; Tier 2 needs partner (Y Combinator, Techstars, etc.).

## Required materials (prepare once, use for all)

- Founder bio (3-4 sentences)
- Company / product description (1 paragraph)
- Tech stack you're using
- Why you need AI credits (1 paragraph — be specific about what you'd build)
- Website URL (your Corpify-built site works!)
- Pitch deck (optional but boosts approval — even a simple 5-slide one)
- Funding status (bootstrapped is fine, just state it honestly)

## What we honestly tell you

- **Approval is NOT guaranteed.** Programs change criteria. Apply, accept rejections, move on.
- **Read each program's current terms.** What we wrote is accurate as of June 2026 but they update without notice.
- **Don't lie on applications.** Disqualification is permanent.
- **Don't apply 20 times** if rejected — wait the cooldown they specify.

## After approval

- Save credit codes immediately (some have short activation windows)
- Set up usage monitoring (programs notify before credit exhaustion)
- Plan next steps for when credits expire (renew? switch providers? raise revenue?)
